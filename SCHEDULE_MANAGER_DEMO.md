# Schedule Manager Demo Notes

Use this branch for the student-worker meeting demo.

## Demo Flow

1. Sign in as staff: `staff@ei.asu.edu` / `edson-staff`.
2. Start on `Schedules` and show the weekly coverage board.
3. Point out the `Coverage Alerts` cards. Each card means a space is open during business hours but no student is scheduled for part or all of that time.
4. Add or change one student schedule block and show the alerts update.
5. Open `Students` to show skill tracking and primary spaces.
6. Open `Spaces` to show business hours by location.
7. Sign out and sign in as a student: `aarav-kapoor@ei.asu.edu` / `edson-student`.
8. Show that the student can see shared schedules but only edit their own availability and skills.

## Short Explanation

This version is intentionally focused on schedules first: who is working, where they are assigned, and whether any Edson E+I space has a coverage gap. Task assignment can build on this later once the scheduling foundation feels right.
