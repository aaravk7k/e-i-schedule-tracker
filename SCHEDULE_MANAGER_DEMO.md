# Schedule Manager Demo Notes

Use this branch for the student-worker meeting demo.

## Demo Flow

1. Sign in as staff: `staff@ei.asu.edu` / `edson-staff`.
2. Start on `Schedules` and show the weekly coverage board.
3. Point out the `Smart Coverage Alerts` cards. Each card means a space is open during business hours but no student is scheduled for part or all of that time.
4. Show the recommended students under each gap. The match is based on primary space, existing schedule, and coverage/event skills.
5. Add or change one student schedule block and show the alerts update.
6. Show `Staff Schedules` so supervisors can see where staff are expected to be.
7. Open `Events`, add an after-hours event, and show that the system asks the best-fit students to cover it.
8. Sign out and sign in as a student: `aarav-kapoor@ei.asu.edu` / `edson-student`.
9. Open `Coverage Requests`, accept or deny the request, then use `Add to My Schedule` after accepting.
10. Sign back in as staff and show the supervisor alert plus the updated schedule board.

## Short Explanation

This version is intentionally focused on smart scheduling: who is working, where staff are located, which spaces have gaps, and which student workers can cover after-hours events. Task assignment can build on this later once the scheduling foundation feels right.
