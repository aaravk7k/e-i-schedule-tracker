# Schedule Manager Demo Notes

Use this branch for the student-worker meeting demo.

## Demo Flow

1. Sign in as staff: `staff@ei.asu.edu` / `edson-staff`.
2. Start on `Coverage` and show the weekly schedule board from the PBIS May 25-May 31 schedule.
3. Point out `Needs Attention`: business-hour coverage gaps and after-hours booking needs are separated so staff can act quickly.
4. Open `Bookings` and show that the tracker has imported May, June, and July bookings, but only shows the selected week.
5. Change the focus date to June or July and show the corrected spaces: ACIC is Chandler, Mesa is shown as The Studios, SkySong operations coverage is 9:00AM-5:00PM, and 1951@SkySong is 8:00AM-5:00PM.
6. Show that after-hours events create one request for the student managing that space.
7. Add a quick after-hours event and show that student requests are created automatically.
8. Sign out and sign in as a student: `aarav-kapoor@ei.asu.edu` / `edson-student`.
9. Open `Requests`, accept or deny a request, and point out the projected weekly hours.
10. If the request would go over the weekly limit, use `Edit My Schedule`, adjust/remove a block, then return to `Requests` and use `Add to My Schedule`.
11. Sign back in as staff and show the supervisor alert plus the updated weekly board.
12. To demo a coverage gap, remove one schedule block from `Quick Schedule Edit`; the `Needs Attention` panel will immediately show the missing time and a suggested student.

## Short Explanation

This version is intentionally simple: who is working, where staff are located, whether each space is covered, which students can cover after-hours events, and whether they still fit under the weekly student-worker limit. Task assignment can build on this later once the scheduling foundation feels right.
